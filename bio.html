taylor.txt

"taylor.txt" and Roach Boarder Productions are part of an electronic music project created by independent producer and songwriter Taylor Stoddard in 2019. The following releases range from dreamy and nostalgic EDM ("Everything Happens") to chaotic distorted noise ("MHM"), all while incorporating ideas inspired by experimental practices in music concrete, granular synthesis, and other unconventional, programming-based methods.

With the release of "Mouthful!", the project expanded to include cheeky and satirical electro-pop. The upcoming album, "Fine Dining", aims to take this futher by making a parody of today's culture of overconsumption with a respect for serious musicianship, the culture paired with it, and the joy of creating music.
